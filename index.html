Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you certify us for Cyber Essentials?

No. Certification is issued by a Certification Body licensed by IASME. We prepare you against the five controls and refer you to one — and if you are not ready we will say so rather than putting in an assessment that fails.

What do we need to buy?

Usually less than you expect. The commonest failures are unsupported software still in use and everyone running as administrator, and neither is fixed by a purchase. We work through configuration first.

Will this stop us being hacked?

No, and nobody can promise that. Good controls make an incident much less likely and far less damaging. Anyone guaranteeing you will not be breached is selling something other than security.

We have backups, so we are covered?

Only if someone has restored one. Ransomware encrypts backups it can reach, so we test a restore in front of you and check that at least one copy is genuinely offline or immutable.

Who tells the ICO if we have a breach?

You do. You are the data controller, and a reportable breach must be notified without undue delay and within 72 hours where feasible. We can help establish what happened technically, but the duty stays with you.

Will you test our systems?

Only with your written authorisation, and only against systems you own or are entitled to authorise. Testing anything else would be unlawful regardless of who asked.
