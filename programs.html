Program 01 · Mostly doing, not buying

Cyber Essentials readiness

Most small organisations fail on the same two things: unsupported software still in use, and everyone working as a local administrator. Neither needs a purchase. We work through the controls, fix what is fixable, and tell you honestly when you are not ready rather than submitting an assessment that fails.

Program 02 · Admin rights first

Security review and patching

The dull controls prevent most incidents: keep software patched, remove administrator rights from daily accounts, turn off what nobody uses, and delete the accounts of people who left two years ago. We go through those rather than selling a product that sits on top of the problem.

  • 2 to 4 weeks
  • Small businesses
  • Fixed fee
  • System access

Program 03 · A restore you have seen

Backup and recovery testing

Almost everyone has backups. Far fewer have ever restored one, and ransomware encrypts backups it can reach. We test a restore in front of you, check that at least one copy is genuinely offline or immutable, and give you an honest recovery time rather than a reassuring one.

  • 2 to 3 weeks
  • Small businesses
  • Fixed fee
  • Backup access
